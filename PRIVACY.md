# Privacy Policy and Terms of Use

## Privacy

WhatStash does not collect, store or share any personal data.

* **No internet access.** The app does not request the `INTERNET` permission, so it cannot send anything off your device.
* **No analytics, ads, accounts or trackers.**
* **Statuses** are read only from the WhatsApp folders you grant access to, and saved copies stay on your device.
* **Deleted-message recovery** is optional. It only works if you grant notification access, and the messages it keeps are stored only on your device. You can delete them, or revoke access, at any time.
* **Links** on the About screen (source code, issue tracker, releases) open in your browser. WhatStash itself does not connect to them.
