WhatStash saves the photo and video statuses your WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business contacts post, so you can keep them after they expire.

It is private by design: WhatStash has no internet permission, no ads, no analytics and no accounts. It only reads the status files WhatsApp has already stored on your phone, from a folder you choose to share.

Features:

* WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business statuses (images and videos)
* Built-in image viewer and video player with playback speed
* Save, share and select multiple statuses at once
* Chat with a number without saving it as a contact
* Optional recovery of deleted messages, stored only on your device
* Material You on Android 12+, night mode and black theme
* Supports Android 7.0 and newer
* No watermark, free and open source (GPL-3.0)

WhatStash is based on WhatSave by Christians Martínez A.
